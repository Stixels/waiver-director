Open **Settings** in the sidebar. Only Owners can change the workspace name, logo, photo capture, or delete the workspace.

## General

- **Workspace name**: shown in the sidebar, in customers' inboxes, and on your waiver page.
- **Workspace URL handle**: the stable path used in your app URLs. It is generated when the workspace is created and cannot be changed.

## Branding

Upload a **workspace logo**. It appears on your public waiver page, in customer emails, and across the app.

## Photo capture

Ask signers for photos on your public waiver page. Each option can be **Off**, **Optional**, or **Required**:

- **Participant photo**: a photo of the signer's face, so staff can match guests at check-in. On phones it opens the front camera.
- **Photo ID**: a photo of a driver's license, passport, or other government ID. On phones it opens the rear camera.

Both are off by default. Changes apply to new signatures right away; existing submissions keep the photos they were signed with. Photos are saved with the signed submission and are visible to everyone on your team in the [submission detail](/guides/submissions-and-customers#submissions).

## Email

- **Email identity**: what customers see in their inbox and where replies go. Verify a reply-to address here before sending [follow-up emails](/guides/follow-up-emails).
- **Custom sending domain**: coming soon. It will let you send from your own domain instead of the shared Waiver Director domain.

## Danger zone

**Delete workspace** permanently removes the workspace. Public waiver links stop working and the team loses access. This cannot be undone.
