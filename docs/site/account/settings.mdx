Open **Settings** in the sidebar. Only Owners can change the workspace name, logo, or delete the workspace.

## General

- **Workspace name**: shown in the sidebar, in customers' inboxes, and on your waiver page.
- **Workspace URL handle**: the stable path used in your app URLs. It is generated when the workspace is created and cannot be changed.

## Branding

Upload a **workspace logo**. It appears on your public waiver page, in customer emails, and across the app.

## Email

- **Email identity**: what customers see in their inbox and where replies go. Verify a reply-to address here before sending [follow-up emails](/guides/follow-up-emails).
- **Custom sending domain**: coming soon. It will let you send from your own domain instead of the shared Waiver Director domain.

## Guests

Only Owners can change these rules.

- **Returning guests**: choose **Sign every visit** (the default) or **Reuse a valid waiver**. With reuse on, a booking's waiver link shows **Use my waiver on file**: the guest enters the email and date of birth they signed with, and their earlier signed waiver counts toward the booking without a new signature. The original signed waiver is never changed.
  - **Waivers expire**: how many days after signing a waiver stays valid. Turn it off for no time limit.
  - **Ask again when I publish a new waiver version**: guests who signed an older version sign the new one. Recommended.
- **Unsigned guest reminders**: email the booking's lead customer a set number of hours (1 to 168) before the booking when some guests still haven't signed. Each booking gets at most one reminder, with its signing link. Reminders need the Pro plan and a verified reply-to address under **Email**.

## Danger zone

**Delete workspace** permanently removes the workspace. Public waiver links stop working and the team loses access. This cannot be undone.
