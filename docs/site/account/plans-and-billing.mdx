Trials include the Pro features.

## Primary workspace on Pro

Pro features apply to one **primary workspace**. If you own more than one workspace on Pro, choose which one is primary under **Plan usage** in the account menu. You can switch your primary workspace once per billing period. Other workspaces stay paused on Pro features until you make them primary or upgrade to Business, which unlocks every workspace you own.

## Canceling

If you cancel, paid features stay available until the end of the current billing period, then the account returns to Free. Your existing signed submissions are kept.
