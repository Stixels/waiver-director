Waiver Director is a waiver operations platform for businesses that run activities, tours, rentals, classes, and other booked experiences. It gives each business a private **workspace** where the team builds a waiver, shares it with customers, tracks who has signed for each booking, and sends follow-up emails after the experience.

## What you can do

- **Build one waiver per workspace** with rich-text terms and custom fields (text, checkbox, dropdown, and date).
- **Publish frozen versions** so every signature is tied to the exact wording the signer accepted.
- **Share the waiver** with a link, a QR code, an embed snippet, or a booking-specific link.
- **Review signed submissions** and customer visit history.
- **Track bookings** synced from Bookeo and spot participants who have not signed yet.
- **Send follow-up emails** after each experience, with an optional AI review of your email copy.
- **Measure** submissions, booking completion, and email activity in analytics.
