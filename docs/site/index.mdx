Waiver Director is a waiver operations platform for businesses that run activities, tours, rentals, classes, and other booked experiences. You use it at [waiverdirector.com](https://www.waiverdirector.com/app) in any web browser. Each business gets a private **workspace** where the team builds a waiver, shares it with customers, tracks who has signed for each booking, and sends follow-up emails after the experience.

## Choose your path

### I'm an owner setting up Waiver Director

1. [Create your account](/get-started/create-your-account)
2. [Create your workspace](/get-started/create-your-workspace)
3. [Choose your plan](/get-started/choose-your-plan)
4. [Publish your first waiver](/get-started/publish-your-first-waiver)
5. [Collect your first signature](/get-started/collect-your-first-signature)

Then [connect Bookeo](/integrations/bookeo) so signatures line up with your bookings, and [set up follow-up emails](/guides/follow-up-emails).

### I'm on staff, running the day's bookings

1. Check the [Dashboard](/guides/dashboard) for today's bookings and the latest signatures.
2. Open [Bookings](/guides/bookings) and look for bookings with a **waiver gap**.
3. Share the [QR code or booking link](/guides/share-and-collect) with anyone who still needs to sign.
4. Look up a signer in [Submissions and customers](/guides/submissions-and-customers) when you need their record.
