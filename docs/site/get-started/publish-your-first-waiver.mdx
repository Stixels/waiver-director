## Before you begin

- Your plan is active. See [Choose your plan](/get-started/choose-your-plan).
- Have your waiver wording ready to paste in.

## Build and publish

1. Open **Waiver** in the sidebar.
2. Give the waiver a title, then paste your terms into the waiver copy.
3. Add any custom fields signers should fill in, such as a required checkbox or a "How did you hear about us?" dropdown.
4. Click **Publish** and confirm. The current draft becomes a frozen published version, and your waiver link goes live.

Later edits stay in the draft until you publish again, and every signature keeps the exact version its signer accepted. See [Build and publish a waiver](/guides/build-and-publish) for details.
