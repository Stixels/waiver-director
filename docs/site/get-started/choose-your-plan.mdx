You can build your waiver on the Free plan. To publish it and collect signatures, you need **Pro** or **Business**, or a trial.

## Before you begin

- You have created a workspace.

## Upgrade

1. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar to open the account menu.
2. Choose **Billing plans**.
3. Pick **Pro** or **Business** and complete checkout, or start a trial if one is offered.

To check your plan afterward, choose **Plan usage** from the same menu. It shows the plan's status and its renewal or trial end date.

## Which plan?

- **Pro** unlocks publishing, booking integrations, follow-up emails, analytics, and team access for one **primary workspace**.
- **Business** unlocks the same features in every workspace you own.

See [Plans and billing](/account/plans-and-billing) for the full comparison.
