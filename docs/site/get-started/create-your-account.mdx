The goal of this step is to sign in to Waiver Director with a verified account.

## Before you begin

You need one of these:

- An email address you can check right now, and a password
- A Google account

## Create the account

1. Go to [waiverdirector.com](https://www.waiverdirector.com) and click **Start for free** in the top-right corner. You can also go straight to the [sign-up page](https://www.waiverdirector.com/sign-up).

<Frame caption="The Waiver Director home page. Start for free in the top-right corner opens the sign-up page.">
	<img
		src="/images/app/website-start-for-free.png"
		alt="Waiver Director home page with the Start for free button highlighted in the top-right corner"
	/>
</Frame>

2. On the **Create your account** page, enter your email address in **Email**, then type a password in **Password** and again in **Confirm password**.
3. Click **Create account**.

<Frame caption="The sign-up page. Fill in the three fields on the right and click Create account, or click Continue with Google.">
	<img
		src="/images/app/sign-up-page.png"
		alt="Create your account page with Email, Password, and Confirm password fields, a Create account button, and Continue with Google"
	/>
</Frame>

4. Check your inbox for a verification code from Waiver Director and enter it. You're signed in once the code is accepted.

To use Google instead, click **Continue with Google** on the [sign-up page](https://www.waiverdirector.com/sign-up) and choose your Google account.

## If you can't sign in

Go to the [sign-in page](https://www.waiverdirector.com/sign-in), or click **Sign in** at the top of [waiverdirector.com](https://www.waiverdirector.com).

<Frame caption="The sign-in page. Forgot password? sits under the password field.">
	<img
		src="/images/app/sign-in-page.png"
		alt="Welcome back sign-in page with Email and Password fields, a Forgot password link, a Sign in button, and Continue with Google"
	/>
</Frame>

- **No code arrived:** check your spam folder, then request a new code.
- **Forgot your password:** click **Forgot password?** on the [sign-in page](https://www.waiverdirector.com/sign-in), or go straight to [reset your password](https://www.waiverdirector.com/forgot-password).
- **Signed up with Google:** use **Continue with Google** on the [sign-in page](https://www.waiverdirector.com/sign-in), not the email and password form.
- **Asked for a second code:** if you turned on two-step verification, enter the code from your authenticator app, text message, or a backup code.

<Frame caption="The reset password page. Enter the email on your account and click Send reset code, then enter the code from your inbox and choose a new password.">
	<img
		src="/images/app/reset-password-page.png"
		alt="Reset your password page with an Email field and a Send reset code button"
	/>
</Frame>
