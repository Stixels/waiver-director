The goal of this step is to sign in to Waiver Director with a verified account.

## Before you begin

You need one of these:

- An email address you can check right now, and a password
- A Google account

## Create the account

1. Go to [waiverdirector.com/sign-up](https://www.waiverdirector.com/sign-up).
2. Enter your email address and a password, then confirm the password.
3. Check your inbox for a verification code and enter it.

To use Google instead, click **Continue with Google** on the [sign-up page](https://www.waiverdirector.com/sign-up) and choose your Google account.

## If you can't sign in

- **No code arrived:** check your spam folder, then request a new code.
- **Forgot your password:** click **Forgot password?** on the [sign-in page](https://www.waiverdirector.com/sign-in), or go straight to [reset your password](https://www.waiverdirector.com/forgot-password).
- **Signed up with Google:** use **Continue with Google** on the [sign-in page](https://www.waiverdirector.com/sign-in), not the email and password form.
- **Asked for a second code:** if you turned on two-step verification, enter the code from your authenticator app, text message, or a backup code.
