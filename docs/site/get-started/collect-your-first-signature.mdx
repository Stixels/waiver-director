## Before you begin

- Your waiver is published. See [Publish your first waiver](/get-started/publish-your-first-waiver).
- Have a phone handy for testing.

## Sign it yourself

1. On the **Waiver** page, click **Show QR code**.
2. Scan it with your phone and fill out the waiver as a customer would.
3. Submit it, then open **Submissions** in the app. Your test signature appears at the top.

	<img src="/images/app/waiver-qr-code.png" alt="Waiver QR code with a copy link button" />
</Frame>

## Share it with customers

- Put the waiver link in booking confirmation emails.
- Print the QR code for your front desk.
- Embed the form on your website.

See [Share and collect signatures](/guides/share-and-collect) for each option.
