---
title: 'Create your workspace'
description: 'Set up the workspace that holds your waiver, bookings, and customers.'
---

A workspace holds everything for one business or location: its waiver, bookings, signed submissions, customers, and emails. Nothing is shared between workspaces.

## Before you begin

- You are signed in to Waiver Director.
- You know the business name customers should see.

## Create the workspace

1. Go to the [sign-in page](https://www.waiverdirector.com/sign-in), enter your email and password (or click **Continue with Google**), and click **Sign in**. The first time you sign in, Waiver Director asks you to create a workspace.
2. Enter your **workspace name**, such as your business name. Customers see it on your waiver page and in emails.
3. Click **Create workspace**. You land on its dashboard.

You become the workspace's **Owner**.

<Note>
	The workspace URL handle is generated from the name when you create it and can't be changed later.
	You can rename the workspace at any time in [Settings](/account/settings).
</Note>

## Add your logo

Open **Settings → Branding** and upload your logo. It appears on your public waiver page, in customer emails, and in the app.
