---
title: 'Quickstart'
description: 'Go from a new account to your first signed waiver.'
---

<Steps>
	<Step title="Create an account">
		Sign up for Waiver Director. You can sign in with email or a supported social provider.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Create a workspace">
		A workspace holds everything for one business or location: its waiver, bookings, submissions,
		customers, and emails. Give it your business name, since customers see it on the waiver page and
		in emails. The workspace's URL handle is generated for you and cannot be changed later.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Write your waiver">
		Open **Waiver** in the sidebar. Add a title, your waiver terms, and any custom fields you want
		signers to fill in. See [Build and publish a waiver](/guides/build-and-publish).
	</Step>
	<Step title="Publish">
		Publishing freezes the current draft as a waiver version and makes the public waiver link live.
		Publishing requires a [Pro plan or trial](/account/plans-and-billing).
	</Step>
	<Step title="Share the link">
		Copy the waiver link, show the QR code at your front desk, or embed the form on your website.
		See [Share and collect signatures](/guides/share-and-collect).
	</Step>
	<Step title="Review the signature">
		Once someone signs, the record appears under **Submissions** and the signer appears under
		**Customers**.
	</Step>
</Steps>
