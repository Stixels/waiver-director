---
title: 'Mailchimp'
description: 'Add waiver signers to a Mailchimp audience automatically.'
---

Connect Mailchimp to add signer contacts to one of your Mailchimp audiences after they sign.

## Connect

<Steps>
	<Step title="Authorize Mailchimp">
		As an Owner, open **Integrations**, select **Mailchimp**, and click **Authorize Mailchimp**.
		Sign in and approve access in Mailchimp.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Choose an audience">Pick the Mailchimp audience signers should be added to.</Step>
</Steps>

If a contact sync fails, the Mailchimp panel shows a notice so you can check the connection.

Owners can disconnect Mailchimp at any time from the Integrations page.
