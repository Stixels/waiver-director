Connecting Bookeo imports your bookings into Waiver Director, keeps them up to date as they change, and gives each booking its own waiver link.

<Note>
	Booking integrations require a Pro plan or trial. On Pro, they work in your primary workspace; on
	Business, in every workspace. See [Plans and billing](/account/plans-and-billing).
</Note>

## Connect

<Steps>
	<Step title="Open Integrations">As an Owner, open **Integrations** and select **Bookeo**.</Step>
	<Step title="Authorize in Bookeo">
		Click **Connect with Bookeo**. Bookeo asks you to approve the permissions Waiver Director needs,
		then returns you to the app.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Initial sync">
		Waiver Director starts importing your bookings. They appear on the [Bookings](/guides/bookings)
		page as the sync completes.
	</Step>
</Steps>

After the initial sync, Bookeo notifies Waiver Director when bookings change, so new bookings, edits, and cancellations flow in automatically.

## Troubleshooting

- **Missing required permissions**: reconnect and approve every permission Bookeo asks for.
- **Integration needs attention**: the connection hit an error. Reconnect from the Integrations page.
- **Owner access required**: ask a workspace Owner to connect the integration.

## Disconnect

Owners can disconnect Bookeo from the Integrations page. Signed submissions keep the booking snapshot captured when they were signed.
