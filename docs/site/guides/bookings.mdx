## Browse bookings

- Step through days with the previous and next day controls.
- Filter by status: All, Active, Incomplete (bookings with a waiver gap), or Canceled.
- Search by customer, activity, or booking number.

Open a booking to see its details, the signed submissions attached to it, and its booking waiver link.

## Waiver gaps

Each booking compares its expected participant count with the participants covered by signed submissions. Each submission covers the signer plus any minors listed on it. When fewer people are covered than expected, the booking has a **waiver gap**, so you can chase missing signatures before the experience starts.

Canceled bookings are excluded from current gaps. Their signed submissions stay available for review.

If [returning guests](/account/settings#guests) can reuse a valid waiver, a guest who confirms their waiver on file also counts toward the booking. They show in the guest list with an **on file** tag, and opening them shows the waiver they originally signed.

## How signatures attach to bookings

Signatures made through a [booking link](/guides/share-and-collect#booking-links) attach to that booking automatically. Each submission stores a snapshot of the booking details at the time of signing, so later changes in your booking provider do not alter the signed record.
