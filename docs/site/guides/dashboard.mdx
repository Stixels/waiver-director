The dashboard is the first page you see in a workspace. It updates in real time as customers sign.

<Frame caption="Today's metrics across the top, upcoming bookings with signed counts, and the follow-up email pipeline. Sample data from a fictional workspace.">
	<img
		src="/images/app/dashboard.png"
		alt="Dashboard with metric cards for bookings, submissions, follow-ups, and new customers above a bookings list and an email follow-ups panel"
	/>
</Frame>

- **Key metrics** with sparklines for recent submissions and activity.
- **Live panel** with today's upcoming bookings and the latest signed submissions.
- **Email pipeline** showing follow-ups that are queued, sent, unscheduled, blocked, or failed.

For trends over a custom date range, open [Analytics](/guides/analytics).

Press <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows) anywhere in the app to search bookings, submissions, and customers in the current workspace.
