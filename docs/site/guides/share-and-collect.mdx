Once your waiver is published, the **Waiver** page gives you several ways to share it.

## Waiver link

**Copy link** gives you the public URL of your waiver. Anyone with the link can sign without an account. Use it in booking confirmations, emails, or text messages.

## QR code

**Show QR code** opens a printable QR code that opens the live waiver. Post it at your front desk or check-in area so walk-in customers can sign on their phones. The **Bookings** page has the same QR code for quick access during check-in.

## Embed on your website

**Copy embed code** gives you an `iframe` snippet that shows the waiver form inside your own site:

```html
<iframe
	src="https://<your-waiver-link>?embed=1"
	title="Waiver form"
	width="100%"
	height="920"
	style="border:0;border-radius:16px;overflow:hidden"
></iframe>
```

## Kiosk mode

**Open kiosk mode** opens a full-screen signing station for a tablet or computer at your front desk. Guests don't need an account, and no one needs to stay signed in on the device.

### Set up a kiosk tablet

1. On the tablet, sign in to Waiver Director and open the **Waiver** page.
2. Select **Open kiosk mode** (the tablet icon). The kiosk opens in a new tab.
3. Sign out of Waiver Director in the other tab if you like. The kiosk keeps working.

Opening the kiosk this way registers that tablet, which is what turns on booking lookup there.

Anyone can open your waiver link followed by `/kiosk`, but without a registered device they can only sign as a walk-in.

If a kiosk tablet is lost or its link gets shared by mistake, an Owner can choose **Reset kiosk links** from the **⋯** menu on the **Waiver** page. Booking lookup then turns off on every kiosk until you open kiosk mode again on each tablet.

### What guests see

From the kiosk start screen, a guest can:

- **I have a booking**: enter their booking number or the email they booked with. The kiosk shows only active bookings that started in the last 3 hours or start in the next 36 hours, with just the activity, start time, and the lead's first name. The guest's signature is attached to the booking they choose.
- **Sign as a walk-in**: sign the waiver without a booking.

To protect each guest's details on a shared device, the kiosk:

- Returns to the start screen 10 seconds after a waiver is submitted, or when the guest taps **Done**.
- Asks "Still there?" after about 75 seconds without a tap or keystroke, and clears the form 15 seconds later.
- Turns off browser autofill, so one guest's name and email are never suggested to the next.
- Limits how many booking searches each kiosk can make in a few minutes. If the limit is reached, guests can still sign as walk-ins.

Use **Toggle full screen** on the start screen to hide the browser bar. For a locked-down device, also turn on your tablet's guided access or kiosk setting.

## Booking links

Each synced booking has its own waiver link. Signatures made through a booking link are attached to that booking automatically, which keeps its [waiver gap](/concepts#waiver-gap) up to date. Copy a booking link from the booking's detail panel on the **Bookings** page and share it with the people on that booking.

## What signers do

1. Open the link or scan the QR code.
2. Fill in their details and any custom fields.
3. Add any minors they are signing for.
4. Read the waiver and draw their signature.
5. Submit. The signed submission is saved permanently.
