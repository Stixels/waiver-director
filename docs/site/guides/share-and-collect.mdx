Once your waiver is published, the **Waiver** page gives you several ways to share it.

## Waiver link

**Copy link** gives you the public URL of your waiver. Anyone with the link can sign without an account. Use it in booking confirmations, emails, or text messages.

## QR code

**Show QR code** opens a printable QR code that opens the live waiver. Post it at your front desk or check-in area so walk-in customers can sign on their phones. The **Bookings** page has the same QR code for quick access during check-in.

## Add it to your website

Open **Settings > Website widget** to get code you can paste into your own site. The widget always shows your live published waiver, so you don't need to change your site when you publish a new version.

- **Button**: a "Sign waiver" button that opens the waiver in a pop-up over your page. Pick the button text and color, then copy the code.
- **Inline form**: the full waiver form inside your page. The frame grows to fit the form, so there's no inner scroll bar.

```html
<script
	src="https://<your-app>/embed.js"
	data-waiver-url="https://<your-waiver-link>"
	data-mode="button"
	data-label="Sign waiver"
	data-color="#111827"
	async
></script>
```

Paste it into a custom HTML or code block, for example on Squarespace, Wix, WordPress, or Webflow.

**Use your own button.** Keep the script on the page and add `data-waiver-director-open` to any link or button. Clicking it opens the waiver in the pop-up. Without the script, the link still opens the waiver page.

```html
<a href="https://<your-waiver-link>" data-waiver-director-open>Sign your waiver</a>
```

**Site builders that block scripts.** Use the plain `iframe` from the same settings page, or **Copy embed code** on the **Waiver** page. It has a fixed height and scrolls inside:

```html
<iframe
	src="https://<your-waiver-link>?embed=1"
	title="Waiver form"
	width="100%"
	height="920"
	style="border:0;border-radius:16px;overflow:hidden"
></iframe>
```

For developers: the widget fires `waiver-director:open`, `waiver-director:close`, and `waiver-director:submitted` events on the button or inline frame, so you can track signatures in your own analytics.

## Booking links

Each synced booking has its own waiver link. Signatures made through a booking link are attached to that booking automatically, which keeps its [waiver gap](/concepts#waiver-gap) up to date. Copy a booking link from the booking's detail panel on the **Bookings** page and share it with the people on that booking.

## What signers do

1. Open the link or scan the QR code.
2. Fill in their details and any custom fields.
3. Add any minors they are signing for. If the waiver has [Minor date of birth](/guides/build-and-publish#minor-date-of-birth) turned on, they also enter each minor's date of birth.
4. Read the waiver and draw their signature.
5. Submit. The signed submission is saved permanently, with an [audit trail](/guides/submissions-and-customers#audit-trail).
