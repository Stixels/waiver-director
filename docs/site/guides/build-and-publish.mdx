## Edit the draft

The waiver builder has three parts:

- **Title**: shown at the top of the public waiver page. Rename it from the page header.
- **Waiver copy**: the terms signers agree to, written in a rich-text editor with headings, lists, links, and alignment.
- **Custom fields**: questions signers answer before signing, on the **Fields** tab of the left panel.
- **Settings**: who can sign and how the public page looks, on the **Settings** tab of the left panel.

Every waiver also collects the signer's details and a drawn signature, and lets an adult add **covered minors** who are signing under the same waiver.

Like other edits, settings take effect once you publish.

### Adult age

**Adult age** is the age of majority where you operate. Guests this age or older sign for themselves. Younger guests are minors, and an adult who signs adds them to the waiver. It defaults to 18. Choose 19 or 21 if your state or province uses that age, for example 19 in Alabama and Nebraska and 21 in Mississippi.

### Minimum signer age

**Minimum signer age** defaults to 18 and can be any whole number from the adult age up to 99, for example 21 for an activity with alcohol. Anyone whose date of birth makes them younger can't submit the waiver themselves, and the check also runs on the server.

It can't be lower than the adult age, because a minor can't sign for themselves. If you raise the adult age above it, the minimum signer age moves up to match. With a minimum above the adult age, guests in between can't take part: they're too old to be added as minors and too young to sign. The editor and the public page both say so.

### Minor date of birth

Turn on **Minor date of birth** to ask for each minor's date of birth. When it's on, every minor needs one and must be under the adult age on the day the waiver is signed. It's off by default, so signers only enter each minor's name.

### Appearance

Choose a **Light** or **Dark** theme for the public waiver page. The preview on the right follows it.

### Custom field types

| Type     | Use it for                                                   |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Text     | Free-form answers, such as an emergency contact              |
| Checkbox | Agreements or yes/no questions                               |
| Dropdown | One choice from a list, such as "How did you hear about us?" |
| Date     | Dates, such as a date of a previous visit                    |

Any field can be marked required. Dropdown answers feed into [analytics](/guides/analytics).

## Publish

Publishing saves the current draft as a new **published waiver version** and makes it the version customers sign. Edits you make after publishing stay in the draft until you publish again.

<Warning>
	Publishing requires a Pro plan or trial. On the Free plan you can build the waiver and preview the
	public signing page, but customers cannot submit it.
</Warning>

## Version history

Open the version history from the waiver page to see every version you have published. Signed submissions always point to the exact version the signer accepted, so older signatures keep their original wording after you republish.
