The **Check-in** page is a front-desk roster for one day. It lists that day's bookings with everyone who has signed, plus walk-in signatures, so staff can mark people as they arrive and see who still needs to sign.

## Use the roster

1. Open **Check-in** from the sidebar. It shows today by default.
2. Step through days with the previous and next day controls, or pick a date from the calendar.
3. Search by participant name, activity, or booking number to find someone quickly.
4. Select **Not arrived** next to a person to mark them **Arrived**. Select it again to undo.

Each signer and each minor on a signed submission has their own row, so you can check in a family one person at a time.

## Daily totals

The totals at the top cover the selected day:

- **Expected**: participants booked on active bookings, plus walk-ins who signed.
- **Signed**: participants covered by signed waivers.
- **Arrived**: participants marked as arrived.
- **Unsigned**: booked participants on active bookings who are not yet covered by a signed waiver.

Canceled bookings are labeled **Canceled** and do not count toward expected or unsigned totals.

## Unsigned guests

When a booking has more participants than signed waivers, it shows an **N unsigned** warning. Send those guests the [booking waiver link](/guides/share-and-collect#booking-links) or point them to the kiosk before they start.

## Walk-ins

Signatures made through a general waiver link or the kiosk without a booking appear under **Walk-ins** on the day they were signed.

## What check-in changes

Arrival marks are operational notes kept separately from the signed waiver. Marking someone as arrived, or undoing it, never changes the signed submission. Owners and Staff can both check participants in.
