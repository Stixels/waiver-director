title: 'Analytics'
description: 'Track submissions, booking completion, customers, and email activity over time.'
---

**Analytics** shows trends for the current workspace over a date range you choose.

<Frame caption="Sample data from a fictional workspace.">
	<img
		src="/images/app/analytics.png"
		alt="Analytics charts for submissions, bookings, email activity, and customers by day"
	/>
</Frame>

- **Submissions**: signed waivers per day, compared with the previous period.
- **Bookings**: bookings per day, compared with the previous period.
- **Customers by day**: new and returning customers who signed each day.
- **Email activity**: totals of sent, queued, failed, and blocked follow-ups, and their status mix.

Analytics only ever includes data from the current workspace.

<Note>Analytics requires a Pro plan or trial.</Note>
