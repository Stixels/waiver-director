## Submissions

<Frame caption="Sample data from a fictional workspace.">

		src="/images/app/signed-records.png"

</Frame>

**Submissions** lists every signed waiver in the workspace, newest first. Search by customer, activity, or booking number, or filter by [tag](#tags). Open a submission to see:

- Signer details and the time they signed
- Custom field answers
- The drawn signature
- Covered minors
- The booking details captured at signing, if the signature came from a booking link
- The follow-up email created from the submission, if any

<Info>
	Signed submissions are permanent records and cannot be edited, by anyone, after they are
	submitted.
</Info>

## Customers

**Customers** groups signers by email address. Search by name or email, or filter by [tag](#tags), then select a customer to see their waiver visit history across all of their submissions in this workspace.

<Frame caption="The customer list with visit counts, and one customer's details and visit history on the right. Sample data from a test workspace.">
	<img
		src="/images/app/customers.png"
		alt="Customers page listing contacts with visit counts and last seen times, with a selected customer's visit history in a side panel"

Customers are scoped to one workspace. The same person signing for two of your workspaces appears separately in each.

## Tags

Tags are short labels, such as _VIP_, _Birthday party_, or _Medical follow-up_, that you add to customers and submissions so you can find and group them later.

- **Add a tag:** Select a customer, or open a submission, then choose **Add tag**. Pick an existing tag or type a new name and press Enter to create it. Each tag gets a color automatically.
- **Remove a tag:** Select the **×** on the tag.
- **Filter by tag:** On **Customers** or **Submissions**, open the **All tags** menu next to the search box and choose a tag. The list shows only records with that tag, most recently tagged first, and the menu shows how many records use each tag. You can combine a tag filter with a search.

Customer tags and submission tags are separate: tagging a customer does not tag their submissions, and the reverse. Tags belong to one workspace.

<Info>
	Tags are stored alongside a submission, not inside it. Adding or removing a tag never changes the
	signed waiver.
</Info>

Owners can also change a tag's color (select the color dot in the **All tags** menu) or delete a tag (select the trash icon). Deleting a tag removes it from every customer and submission. A workspace can have up to 100 tags, and each customer or submission up to 20.
