**Submissions** lists every signed waiver in the workspace, newest first. Search by customer, activity, or booking number. Open a submission to see:

- Signer details and the time they signed
- Custom field answers
- The drawn signature
- Covered minors and their dates of birth
- The booking details captured at signing, if the signature came from a booking link
- The follow-up email created from the submission, if any

## Audit trail

Below the signed document, each submission shows an **Audit trail** you can point to if a signature is ever disputed:

- **Received by server**: when Waiver Director recorded the signature.
- **IP address**: the address the signature came from, recorded by the server. The signer can't change it.
- **Device / browser**: the browser and device the signer's browser reported.
- **Document hash**: a SHA-256 fingerprint of the exact waiver version the signer saw. Two submissions with the same document hash signed identical text.
- **Record hash**: a SHA-256 fingerprint of the signed record (signer details, answers, minors, and signature). Any later change to the record would no longer match it.
- **Form opened** and **Signature drawn**: times reported by the signer's browser, for context.

Waivers signed before the audit trail was added show "No audit trail was recorded."

## Customers

**Customers** groups signers by email address. Search by name or email, then select a customer to see their waiver visit history across all of their submissions in this workspace.

<Frame caption="The customer list with visit counts, and one customer's details and visit history on the right. Sample data from a test workspace.">

Customers are scoped to one workspace. The same person signing for two of your workspaces appears separately in each.

## Export to CSV

Owners can download every record in the workspace as a CSV file that opens in Excel, Google Sheets, or Numbers. Select **Export CSV** at the top of **Submissions** or **Customers**. Staff don't see the button, and the server refuses export requests from anyone who isn't an Owner.

- **Submissions** export one row per signed waiver, oldest first: signer details, signing time, covered minors, the waiver version signed, booking details captured at signing, marketing consent, and one column per custom field. Answers are labeled the way the signer saw them in the version they signed.
- **Customers** export one row per customer: name, email, first and last seen, and visit count.

Signing, first-seen, and last-seen times are in UTC. Booking start times are exported as the booking provider sent them. The export includes the whole workspace, not only the current search. Drawn signatures aren't included; open the submission to see one.
