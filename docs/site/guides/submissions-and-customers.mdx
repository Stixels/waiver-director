## Submissions

**Submissions** lists every signed waiver in the workspace, newest first. Search by customer, activity, or booking number. Open a submission to see:

- Signer details and the time they signed
- Custom field answers
- The drawn signature
- Covered minors
- The booking details captured at signing, if the signature came from a booking link
- The follow-up email created from the submission, if any

## Signed PDF copies

On Pro and Business, every signed submission can be turned into a PDF. The PDF contains the waiver text exactly as signed, the signer's answers, covered minors, booking details, marketing consent, and the drawn signature. A **signature record** at the end lists the submission ID, the signing time in UTC, and a SHA-256 fingerprint of the signed record, so you can show later that a copy matches what is stored.

- **Automatic copy to the signer**: right after someone signs, they get an email with their signed PDF attached. It is sent from your workspace name, and replies go to your verified reply-to address if you have one. Owners can turn this off under **Settings > Email > Signed copies**.
- **Email copy to signer**: open a submission and select **Email copy to signer** to send it again, for example when a customer can't find the first email. The copy only ever goes to the signer's email on record. The submission header shows when the last copy was sent.
- **Download PDF**: Owners can select **Download PDF** on any submission to save the signed copy, for example to answer a dispute or insurance request.

## Customers

**Customers** groups signers by email address. Search by name or email, then select a customer to see their waiver visit history across all of their submissions in this workspace.

<Frame caption="The customer list with visit counts, and one customer's details and visit history on the right. Sample data from a test workspace.">
	<img
		src="/images/app/customers.png"
		alt="Customers page listing contacts with visit counts and last seen times, with a selected customer's visit history in a side panel"

</Frame>

Customers are scoped to one workspace. The same person signing for two of your workspaces appears separately in each.
